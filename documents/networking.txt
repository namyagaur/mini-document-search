Networking is the practice of connecting computers and devices so they can share information and resources. It involves hardware like routers, switches, and cables, as well as software protocols that manage communication. Networks can be small, like a home Wi-Fi setup, or large, like the internet. The two main types are Local Area Networks (LANs), which connect devices in a limited area, and Wide Area Networks (WANs), which span across cities or countries. Networking enables email, file sharing, online gaming, and cloud services. Protocols such as TCP/IP ensure data is transmitted reliably. Security measures like firewalls and encryption protect networks from cyber threats. Overall, networking is the backbone of modern communication, allowing people and systems worldwide to stay connected.
