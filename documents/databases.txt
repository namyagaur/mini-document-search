Databases are organized collections of data that allow easy storage, retrieval, and management of information. They are used to handle large amounts of structured data efficiently. A database is managed by a Database Management System (DBMS), which provides tools for creating, updating, and querying data. Common types of databases include relational databases, which use tables and SQL, and NoSQL databases, which handle unstructured or semi-structured data. Databases are essential in applications like banking, e-commerce, healthcare, and social media. They ensure data consistency, security, and availability. Modern databases also support cloud storage and distributed systems, making them scalable for global use. Overall, databases are the backbone of most software systems, enabling smooth data-driven operations.
